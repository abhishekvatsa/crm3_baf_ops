Your account

Request account deletion.

Ask Abhishek Vatsa, publisher and owner, to delete your CRM-III BAF Ops account and associated personal data. You do not need to sign in, have approved access or reinstall the app.

How to request it

  1. Email email.abhishekvatsa@gmail.com, preferably from the Google email address used in the app.
  2. Use the subject CRM-III BAF Ops account deletion and state that you want your app account and associated personal data deleted.
  3. If you cannot use that email address, explain this. The owner will arrange a proportionate identity check before acting.
Prepare deletion email

This opens an editable email. You review and send it yourself. If no mail app opens, use the address above in your usual mail service. Never include your password or a verification code.

What happens next

The owner verifies the request, identifies the account and associated personal data, and replies with the scope and expected completion time. The request covers the app account, profile, notification registrations and associated personal data, including copies in work history, audit and acceptance records; it is not merely a request to suspend access. The owner carries out the required backend deletion steps and requests applicable deletion from service providers.

Shared maintenance, safety, approval and audit records may involve other people and equipment. If specific information must be retained for a justified safety, security or legal reason, the owner will explain what remains, why, and the applicable retention period. Remaining personal attribution will be reviewed for removal or anonymisation where it is no longer needed. Operational records are not automatically exempt from deletion review.

This request does not delete your Google account. The owner confirms the outcome by email, including any retained information and further device steps.

Saved work on your phone

Signing out, uninstalling the app or losing approval does not itself delete the server account. Uninstalling or clearing app storage can erase work that has not reached the server.

If you have unsent work, mention this in your request so it can be reviewed before any irreversible step. Previously exported reports or copies held by other recipients are separate from the app account; tell the owner if your request concerns those copies too.
