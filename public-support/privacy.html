Privacy policy

How your information is handled.

CRM-III BAF Ops supports authorised workplace maintenance and operations. Abhishek Vatsa is the publisher and owner, and the contact for privacy requests.

Effective date: 27 September 2026 · Contact: email.abhishekvatsa@gmail.com

Information used by the app

  • Account and access: your Google sign-in identifier, name, email address and available profile-photo URL; your app roles, approval status and access history. These identify you and control access. The app does not ask for or store your Google password.
  • Work you record: maintenance issues, observations, responses, comments, equipment and charge references, instructions, approvals and audit history. Actions can include the author's identifier, name and time so authorised people can follow the work.
  • Notifications: a messaging token, an app-installation identifier and notification delivery/readiness information, used to send relevant work and safety notifications. Phone notification permission affects whether alerts can be shown; turning it off does not itself stop notification-token registration.
  • Reliability and security: crash reports, stack traces, device/operating-system and app-version information, installation/session identifiers, and limited diagnostic context. Production mobile builds enable Firebase Crashlytics automatically; the app currently has no in-app diagnostics opt-out. The app supplies a hashed user identifier and role/approval context for troubleshooting. Firebase/Google services also process technical request and security information.

Account information is needed for sign-in and access review. Work data is created when you use the corresponding features. Avoid entering unrelated personal or sensitive information into free-text work records.

Purpose and access

Information is used to authenticate users, assign and follow up work, maintain equipment histories, produce reports, deliver notifications, protect access and diagnose failures. Approved users see records according to their roles; administrators manage access and investigate problems.

Google/Firebase services provide authentication, the Cloud Firestore database, server processing, messaging and crash reporting. Data may be processed on service-provider infrastructure outside your location. The app does not use an advertising SDK or sell personal data.

Reports can include names, authored observations and the generating user's details. When you choose to share or print a report, the chosen recipient or service receives that copy. Use an appropriate recipient and protect exported operational information.

Storage and security

The app keeps local records and pending submissions on the device and synchronises supported records with the cloud. Network service connections use encrypted transport. Access controls and account checks restrict protected functions. Android app backup and device-transfer of the app database are disabled; exported files are separate.

Signing out or revoking approval is an access change, not a deletion of all local or server data. No system can guarantee absolute security. Contact the owner if you believe information has been exposed.

Retention and deletion

Account and operational information is retained while needed for the service, follow-up work and justified safety, security or legal obligations. Retention is reviewed when a deletion request is handled; there is no blanket promise that every operational record is either immediately erased or permanently exempt.

You can request deletion of your app account and associated personal data, including while access is pending or unavailable. The owner verifies the request and explains completion, any justified retained information and its retention period. The review includes personal data copied into work history, audit and acceptance records, not just the visible profile. Shared equipment history may remain where it no longer contains personal data or where specific retention is justified; it is not automatically exempt. The owner also reviews applicable service-provider deletion steps. Provider records and previously exported copies may have separate retention or deletion constraints, which will be explained as applicable.

Your choices and contact

You may contact the owner to ask about your information, correct account details, raise a privacy concern or request deletion. You can manage notification permission in your phone settings. The app is intended for authorised workplace users, not children.

Email email.abhishekvatsa@gmail.com. Do not send passwords or verification codes. This policy may be updated when the app or its data handling changes; the effective date identifies the current version.

These support pages contain no analytics scripts, advertising or tracking forms. Firebase Hosting processes ordinary web requests to deliver the pages. Opening an email link uses your chosen mail service and does not send anything automatically.
