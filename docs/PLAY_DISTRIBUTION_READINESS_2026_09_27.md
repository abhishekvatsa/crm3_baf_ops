## Which steps can proceed

This checklist is not a reason to stop source repairs, DEV validation, release preparation or work on the public policy/contact pages. Google permits **internal testing before full app setup is complete**; closed testing requires completed app setup, and public production access has further account-specific requirements. The [official testing-track guidance](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465?hl=en) makes those stages distinct. Current Console eligibility and a suitable signed candidate still need verification; this is not a claim that a release has been uploaded or accepted.

For the intended ordinary Play distribution, the privacy policy, truthful Data safety declarations and applicable account-deletion route are real release requirements. Account deletion does **not** require an immediate destructive button: Google permits an in-app link to a public deletion-request resource, and that resource may offer a monitored support email or request form. Requests must actually be fulfilled; access suspension alone is insufficient. Any legitimate retention must be specifically disclosed and justified, rather than assuming every operational record is exempt. See [Google's deletion guidance](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13327111?hl=en). Its exemption for permanently private and enterprise device-management apps must not be inferred merely from workplace use.

The [20 September preparation record](PLAY_DISTRIBUTION_PREPARATION_2026_09_20.md) contains dated observations, **not current setup counts, tester progress, certificate inventory or production-access status**.

The focused current-source review found no user-facing privacy-policy/contact route or account-deletion request route. Google sign-in creates an app account and pending profile before approval, so this is not an account-free app. Google requires a [privacy policy in the app and Console](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10144311?hl=en) and, for applicable account-creating apps, an [in-app and external deletion-request path](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13327111?hl=en). Workplace use alone does not establish an exemption.

The publisher/owner and public support/privacy/deletion contact are confirmed above. The policy and request-handling procedure still need to describe actual retention and fulfillment; disabling access is not account deletion. Any justified retention of shared operational/audit evidence and treatment of local uncertain submissions must be explicit. No fixed retention period, destructive deletion routine or unimplemented retention promise is established by the contact confirmation.
